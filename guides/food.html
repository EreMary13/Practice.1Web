Eating Well on a Student Budget

Your first year is busy: new classes, new friends, and long days on campus. It is easy to skip meals or spend your allowance on milktea.

Eating regular, simple meal helps you focus in class, and you do not need a bid budget to do it.

Where to Eat Near Campus

You have more options than you think

  • The school canteen for quick meals between classes.
  • Carinderias outside the gate for cheap, filling ulam and rice.
  • Your own baon from home, ehich is the cheapest of all choice.
  • Convenience store for emergency snacks, not for every meal.

How to Plan your Weekly Budget

A little plan on Sunday saves you from being broke on Thursday.

  1. Check how much allowance you have for the whole week.
  2. Set aside money for fare and school needs first.
  3. Divide what is left by the number of school days.
  4. Pack boan on at least two days to stretch your budget.
  5. Keep a small amount for emergencies, like a surprise project.

Quick Tips

Always bring a refillable water bottle. Water stations on campus are free, and you will save a lot compared to buying bottled drinks everyday.
